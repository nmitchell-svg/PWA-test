Send a Push Notification

Send a notification to subscribers for a specific contractor. The contractor ID must match the ID used by that contractor's PWA.

How targeting works
  1. Enter the contractor ID assigned to the website, such as demo9-hvac.
  2. The platform finds only subscriptions with that contractor_id.
  3. Enter the notification and destination URL.
  4. Enter your Netlify ADMIN_SECRET and send.
